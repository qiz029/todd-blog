This series is a set of field notes from doing engineering, leading a team, and hiring during the AI wave. It doesn't predict the future. It records what is changing right now: interview loops that no longer measure the right things, tokens turning into electricity, and the word "engineer" quietly getting redefined.

Read it in order. The question one post leaves open is usually where the next one starts.
