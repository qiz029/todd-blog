status: ongoing
---

Beginning with the birth of the World Wide Web in 1989, this series follows the internet toward 2003 and 2004, as an experimental technology became part of society’s essential infrastructure.

We follow people, products, companies, and technical paths through time, asking what people knew, what they believed, and what choices and constraints they faced. We bring questions from the present, while letting the history lead us to conclusions of its own.
